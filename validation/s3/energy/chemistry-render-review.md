## Chemical review

- Exothermic transfer releases energy to surroundings; products have lower enthalpy/chemical-energy level and negative deltaH. Endothermic transfer has the inverse signs/order. Requiring a spark does not determine overall sign.
- Breaking bonds absorbs energy; formation releases energy. Net reaction sign compares totals; the misconceptions and offered distractors are unambiguously wrong for each stated prompt.
- Activation energy is the minimum collisional energy to react. Catalyst lowers the barrier without changing the specified initial/final states or total enthalpy change. D08 retains both end levels and a second lower peak above both ends.
- Screen y decreases upwards: exothermic p>r, endothermic p<r. Ea is attached r-to-peak; deltaH is attached r-to-p with a single arrowhead. Free endpoints or reversed attachments remain assessably wrong, not malformed. Absent required arrows/labels/axes block assessment; nonfinite/broken graph data also blocks assessment.
